Kajabi is one of the most recognized names in the online course space, but at $149/month for its most basic plan, it prices out a lot of creators before they've even made their first sale. After helping dozens of coaches and course creators migrate platforms, I can tell you clearly: Kajabi is not the only serious option in 2026, and for many creators it isn't even the best one.

## Key Takeaways

- Kajabi's Basic plan starts at $149/month — significantly more expensive than most alternatives
- GoHighLevel at $97/month replaces Kajabi AND adds a full CRM, sales pipelines, and marketing automation
- Teachable and Thinkific are solid pure-play course platforms for creators who don't need CRM features
- The best platform depends on whether you're a solo creator, a coach with a client pipeline, or an agency serving multiple clients
- Most alternatives offer free trials; GoHighLevel offers a 14-day free trial to test everything

## Why Creators Are Looking for Kajabi Alternatives

Kajabi has a strong brand and genuinely good product, but three things push creators to look elsewhere:

**Price:** The entry plan is $149/month with a limit of 3 products and 3 funnels. If you want unlimited products, you're looking at $399/month on the Growth plan. That's a serious commitment for a creator still building their audience.

**Feature ceiling:** Kajabi's CRM is basic. If you run coaching calls, manage leads, follow up on unpaid invoices, or run ads driving people into a sales pipeline — you'll quickly feel the walls closing in.

**Transaction fees:** Kajabi doesn't charge transaction fees on its own plans, but depending on how you connect Stripe or PayPal, you may still hit processing friction that alternatives handle more elegantly.

I've moved clients off Kajabi for all three of these reasons. Let me walk you through the strongest alternatives.

## The Top Kajabi Alternatives for Course Creators in 2026

### 1. GoHighLevel — Best for Coaches and Creators Who Also Want a CRM

GoHighLevel is what I recommend most often to course creators who are also selling coaching, consulting, or any service alongside their digital products. It is not a pure course platform — it's a full business operating system — but that's exactly why it wins for a certain type of creator.

At $97/month (Starter plan) or $297/month (Unlimited), you get:

- Course and membership hosting (Communities feature)
- Unlimited funnels and landing pages
- Email marketing and SMS automation
- A full CRM with pipelines and deal stages
- Appointment booking and calendar integration
- Two-way SMS and email conversations
- AI-powered follow-up tools

Kajabi at $149/month gives you a cleaner course UI out of the box, but GoHighLevel gives you an entire marketing and sales stack that Kajabi simply doesn't have. I've had coaching clients consolidate five separate tools — course platform, CRM, email tool, funnel builder, and appointment scheduler — into GoHighLevel and cut their monthly software spend by $300–$600.

The learning curve is steeper than Kajabi. GoHighLevel is not plug-and-play for someone who just wants to upload a course and collect payments. But if you have a lead gen funnel, a coaching offer alongside your course, or any kind of team following up with leads, there's no better value in 2026.

### 2. Teachable — Best for Beginners and Pure Course Creators

Teachable is the closest thing to a beginner-friendly Kajabi alternative. The free plan lets you publish one course (with an 10% transaction fee), and the Basic plan starts at $39/month with no transaction fees.

What Teachable does well:
- Clean, student-friendly course UI
- Simple checkout and coupon management
- Solid affiliate program management
- Integrated coaching session sales

What Teachable doesn't do:
- CRM or pipeline management
- Advanced email automation (you'll still need ConvertKit or Mailchimp)
- SMS or multi-channel follow-up

If you're a creator who just wants to host and sell a course without complexity, Teachable is my recommendation. If you expect to scale into a coaching business or grow a client pipeline, you'll eventually outgrow it.

### 3. Thinkific — Best for Structured Course Businesses

Thinkific is the polished alternative that often comes up in the same breath as Kajabi. Pricing starts at $36/month (Basic) and goes to $149/month (Expand), which is still cheaper than Kajabi's equivalent tiers.

Thinkific's strengths:
- No transaction fees on any paid plan
- Very clean course builder with strong quiz and assignment tools
- Communities feature built in
- App store with integrations

Thinkific is excellent for structured learning experiences — think certification programs, corporate training, or multi-module flagship courses. It doesn't try to be a CRM or funnel builder, so pair it with an email tool like ActiveCampaign if you need automation.

### 4. Podia — Best for Simplicity Across Multiple Product Types

Podia lets you sell courses, digital downloads, memberships, and webinars from a single dashboard. Pricing starts at $39/month with no transaction fees.

What I like about Podia is its simplicity. You can have a product live in under an hour. It also includes basic email marketing, which removes one tool from your stack. The downside: email and automation features are surface-level, and there's no CRM. For a creator just starting out, that's fine.

### 5. Systeme.io — Best Free Option

Systeme.io deserves a mention purely because it has a genuinely free plan that includes courses, email marketing, funnels, and automation. For a creator with zero budget, this is hard to beat.

The tradeoffs: the platform feels less polished than Kajabi, the course student experience is basic, and support can be slow. But for validating an offer before you invest in a premium tool, Systeme.io is a smart first stop.

## What Kajabi Still Does Better (Honest Assessment)

I'm not here to trash Kajabi. There are things it does genuinely well that most alternatives don't match:

**Student experience:** Kajabi's course player is polished, mobile-friendly, and feels premium. Students notice it. If your brand perception depends on delivering a luxury digital experience, Kajabi's UI is hard to beat out of the box.

**Built-in podcast hosting:** Kajabi includes podcast hosting, which none of the alternatives above match. If you run a podcast as a core part of your content business, Kajabi bundles this cleanly.

**Integrated community (Circle competitor):** Kajabi's community product has improved significantly and rivals standalone tools. For creators running a tight course + community combo, this integration is seamless.

**Simplicity at scale:** Kajabi is genuinely easier to manage when you have a large catalog and a big student base. Everything stays within one interface without needing to configure workflows.

The bottom line: if budget isn't a concern and you're a pure course creator who doesn't need a CRM, Kajabi is still a very solid choice. But for most creators I work with — especially those also selling their time — it charges premium prices for features they don't fully need while lacking features they genuinely do.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q:** What is the cheapest Kajabi alternative that still lets me host courses professionally?
**A:** Thinkific at $36/month and Teachable at $39/month are the cheapest professional-grade options. Both allow unlimited students on paid plans and charge no transaction fees. Systeme.io is free but has a less polished student experience.

**Q:** Can GoHighLevel replace Kajabi completely?
**A:** Yes, for most creators. GoHighLevel's Communities and Courses features cover course hosting, drip content, and membership access. Where it doesn't match Kajabi is in the student-facing polish — Kajabi's course player looks more premium. But GoHighLevel adds a full CRM, email/SMS automation, and funnel builder that Kajabi doesn't include.

**Q:** Does GoHighLevel charge transaction fees on course sales?
**A:** No. GoHighLevel does not charge transaction fees. You connect your own Stripe account and keep all revenue minus Stripe's standard processing fee (typically 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction).

**Q:** Is Kajabi worth $149/month for a beginner course creator?
**A:** In most cases, no. A beginner is better served starting on Teachable's Basic plan ($39/month) or even Systeme.io's free plan to validate their offer before investing in Kajabi. Kajabi's pricing makes more sense once you're generating consistent course revenue and want everything under one roof.

**Q:** What does GoHighLevel cost and what's included?
**A:** GoHighLevel's Starter plan is $97/month and includes CRM, funnels, email/SMS automation, appointment booking, and course/membership hosting for one business location. The Unlimited plan at $297/month removes location limits and adds white-labeling. The Agency Pro (SaaS) plan is $497/month and unlocks SaaS reselling. All plans include a 14-day free trial.

**Q:** Can I migrate my Kajabi courses to another platform easily?
**A:** Course content (videos, PDFs) can be downloaded and re-uploaded to any platform. What doesn't transfer automatically are student enrollments, progress data, and completion certificates. Most platforms have migration guides, and GoHighLevel has community resources to assist. Budget 2–4 hours for a simple course migration.

**Q:** Which Kajabi alternative is best for coaches who also sell courses?
**A:** GoHighLevel is the clear winner for this use case. Coaches need a CRM to track prospects, automated follow-up sequences, appointment booking, and a way to manage the sales conversation — none of which Kajabi handles well. GoHighLevel covers all of this plus course hosting at a lower price point.

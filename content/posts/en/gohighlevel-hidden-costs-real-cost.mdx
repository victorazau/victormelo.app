GoHighLevel starts at $97 per month on the Starter plan, but that number alone will not tell you what you'll actually pay every month. Once you factor in SMS and call usage through Twilio, email sending costs, and a few optional but tempting add-ons, most solo operators and small agencies end up spending somewhere between $150 and $350 per month all-in. I've implemented GoHighLevel for over a dozen clients across different industries, and the cost surprise is the single most common friction point I see — so let me walk you through every line item before you commit.

## Key Takeaways

- GoHighLevel has three base plans: Starter at $97/mo, Unlimited at $297/mo, and Agency Pro (SaaS mode) at $497/mo.
- The base plan does NOT include SMS, call, or email usage — those are billed separately through Twilio and LC Email.
- Most small agencies spend $150–$250/mo all-in; higher-volume operations can push $400–$600/mo.
- There is no hidden fee in the sneaky sense — every cost is documented — but the billing model catches newcomers off guard.
- A 14-day free trial is available so you can test before committing to any plan.

## What the Three Plans Actually Include

Let's start with the base plans, because there is real confusion about what each tier unlocks.

**Starter — $97/mo** is designed for a single business (one sub-account). You get the CRM, pipeline management, two-way SMS and email, funnels, websites, appointment scheduling, reputation management, and basic automation. The Starter plan caps you at one client location, which makes it appropriate for a business owner running their own operation, not an agency managing multiple clients.

**Unlimited — $297/mo** removes the sub-account limit so you can onboard unlimited clients under one roof. You also get the branded desktop app, API access, and a broader set of automation options. This is the plan most agencies start on and it is the one I recommend for anyone managing three or more client accounts.

**Agency Pro / SaaS Mode — $497/mo** adds the ability to resell GoHighLevel as your own SaaS product with custom pricing tiers, automated sub-account provisioning, and a split-billing feature. If you want to understand whether SaaS mode is worth the jump, I covered it in detail in [how agencies make money with GoHighLevel SaaS mode](/blog/how-agencies-make-money-gohighlevel-saas-mode).

None of the three plans include usage-based messaging or email costs. That is the most misunderstood part of the pricing.

## The Twilio and LC Phone Cost Reality

GoHighLevel relies on Twilio (or their native LC Phone system, which also runs on Twilio infrastructure) for all SMS and voice functionality. You connect your own Twilio account or use LC Phone, and GoHighLevel passes the Twilio rates through to you.

Here are the approximate costs you need to budget for:

| Usage Type | Approx. Cost |
|---|---|
| Outbound SMS (US) | ~$0.0079 per segment |
| Inbound SMS (US) | ~$0.0075 per segment |
| Outbound calls (US) | ~$0.014 per minute |
| Inbound calls (US) | ~$0.0085 per minute |
| Local phone number | ~$1.15/mo per number |
| Toll-free number | ~$2.15/mo per number |
| A2P 10DLC registration | $4–$20 one-time |

For a small business sending 1,000 SMS per month and making moderate use of call forwarding, expect to add $15–$40 to your monthly bill from Twilio alone. For a busy agency running lead nurture sequences for five to ten clients, $80–$150/mo in Twilio costs is realistic.

One important note: A2P 10DLC registration is now required in the US for business texting. GoHighLevel will prompt you to register, and you pay Twilio's fee directly. It is a one-time cost per brand/campaign, not monthly — but you need to budget for it up front.

## Email Sending Costs: LC Email vs. External SMTP

GoHighLevel's native email system is called LC Email, and it operates on a credit model. You get a small monthly credit allocation on paid plans, but beyond that you pay per email sent.

The LC Email rate is approximately **$0.001 per email** (roughly $1 per 1,000 emails). For context:

- Sending a campaign to 5,000 contacts once per week = ~20,000 emails/mo = ~$20/mo extra.
- A smaller list of 500 contacts with weekly emails = ~$2/mo, essentially negligible.

Alternatively, you can plug in your own SMTP provider — Mailgun, SendGrid, Postmark, or others — and pay those providers' rates instead. Mailgun's free tier covers 1,000 emails/day for three months; after that, their Flex plan is competitive. Most of my clients with large lists prefer this route because it gives them better deliverability control and often lower per-email costs at volume.

If you are already using a dedicated email provider, connecting it to GoHighLevel is straightforward and I recommend it for anyone with a list over 2,000 contacts.

## Add-Ons That Will Tempt You (And Whether They Are Worth It)

GoHighLevel's marketplace and settings area will surface several paid add-ons. Here is an honest look at the ones you'll encounter:

**AI Conversation (HighLevel AI / Conversation AI)** — Automated SMS and chat responses powered by AI. As of 2026 this is billed per message sent by the AI, roughly $0.02–$0.04 per AI message. For high-volume use cases like 24/7 lead qualification, this can add $30–$80/mo but often replaces a human VA doing repetitive triage. I covered this more in [GoHighLevel AI features in 2026](/blog/gohighlevel-ai-features-2026).

**Content AI** — AI-powered copy generation inside the platform. Billed per word generated; light users will barely notice the cost, heavy users might see $10–$25/mo added.

**Workflow AI** — Helps suggest automation steps. Smaller cost impact, typically under $10/mo for most users.

**WhatsApp Integration** — If your client base is outside North America, WhatsApp messaging is available but carries its own Meta conversation costs on top of GoHighLevel's connector fee. Budget separately for this.

**HIPAA Compliance Add-On** — For healthcare clients who need a BAA (Business Associate Agreement), there is a paid compliance upgrade. If you work with medical or dental practices, factor this in.

None of these add-ons are forced on you. The platform works perfectly well without them for most use cases. But they are genuinely useful features, and it is easy to enable two or three and suddenly realize your bill has crept up.

## Is the Total Cost Actually Good Value?

Honestly? For most agencies and service-based businesses, yes — and by a significant margin.

Consider what GoHighLevel replaces in a typical agency tech stack:

- CRM: HubSpot or Pipedrive ($50–$800/mo depending on tier)
- Email marketing: ActiveCampaign or Mailchimp ($50–$200/mo)
- Funnel builder: ClickFunnels ($97–$297/mo)
- Appointment scheduling: Calendly Pro ($16/mo)
- Reputation management: Birdeye or Podium ($300–$500/mo)
- SMS platform: Twilio or SimpleTexting ($25–$100/mo)

A conservative stack hits $500–$1,000/mo before you even add support overhead. GoHighLevel consolidates all of that for $97–$497/mo plus variable usage costs. The math is favorable in almost every scenario I've modeled.

Where GoHighLevel is NOT the right call: if you are a solopreneur who only needs email marketing and a basic CRM, something like ActiveCampaign at $29/mo or even a free HubSpot tier might be more appropriate. GoHighLevel's power is overkill if you are not going to use the automation, pipeline, and messaging features.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q:** Does GoHighLevel charge per contact or per user?
**A:** GoHighLevel does not charge per contact or per seat on any of its three plans. The Starter ($97/mo) limits you to one sub-account; Unlimited ($297/mo) and Agency Pro ($497/mo) allow unlimited sub-accounts and unlimited team members. Usage costs (SMS, calls, email) are separate and usage-based, but the core platform fee is flat.

**Q:** Is there a setup fee or onboarding fee?
**A:** GoHighLevel itself does not charge a setup fee. You pay the monthly plan fee starting when your 14-day free trial ends. If you hire an implementation specialist (like me) to configure the platform for you, that is a separate service cost — not a GoHighLevel charge.

**Q:** Can I control how much I spend on Twilio each month?
**A:** Yes. You fund your Twilio account with a prepaid balance and can set spending limits. GoHighLevel will use that balance for messaging and calls, and Twilio will not exceed your funded amount without you adding more. This makes it easy to cap your variable costs.

**Q:** What happens to my data if I cancel?
**A:** GoHighLevel allows you to export contacts, pipeline data, and most assets. Funnels can be exported; workflows are not portable to other platforms in a plug-and-play way since the logic is proprietary. The 14-day free trial is a good time to evaluate whether you can live with that lock-in.

**Q:** Is the $97 Starter plan enough for a small agency?
**A:** The Starter plan is designed for a single business, not an agency. If you are managing even two client accounts, you will almost certainly need the Unlimited plan at $297/mo to get multiple sub-accounts. The one exception is if you are running your own business and using GoHighLevel purely for your own lead generation and CRM — in that case, Starter is genuinely sufficient.

**Q:** Are there annual billing discounts?
**A:** GoHighLevel has offered discounts for annual billing in the past (typically equivalent to getting two months free). This offer varies and may not always be publicly available. It is worth checking during the checkout or contacting support after your trial to ask about annual pricing.

**Q:** Does the free trial require a credit card?
**A:** Yes, GoHighLevel requires a credit card to start the 14-day free trial. You are not charged until the trial ends, and you can cancel before that point without being billed.

## Conclusion

GoHighLevel's pricing is transparent if you know where to look — but the total cost of ownership is genuinely different from the headline $97 or $297 number. Between Twilio usage, email sending, and the occasional useful add-on, most real-world bills land between $150 and $400 per month depending on plan and volume.

For an agency or service business that would otherwise be paying for five to eight separate tools, that number is almost always a bargain. For a solopreneur with light needs, it may be more than necessary.

My honest recommendation: start the 14-day free trial, connect a Twilio account with a small prepaid balance, and run your actual workflows for two weeks. You will have a precise picture of your real monthly cost before you ever pay a full bill — and that is the only way to know whether GoHighLevel is the right fit for your specific situation.

## What Do Coaches and Consultants Actually Need from a CRM?

A CRM (Customer Relationship Management) system for coaches and consultants is software that manages leads, clients, communications, and sales pipelines — ideally in one place. But coaching businesses have specific needs that generic CRMs often miss:

- **Appointment scheduling** integrated with your calendar and automated reminders
- **Sales funnels and landing pages** to capture leads from ads or organic traffic
- **Email and SMS automation** for nurture sequences and onboarding
- **Pipeline visibility** to see where every prospect stands
- **Course or membership hosting** if you sell digital products alongside 1:1 services
- **Invoicing and payment collection** tied to the same system

Most coaches in 2026 are duct-taping 4–6 separate tools together. I've seen this exact setup dozens of times: Calendly for booking, Mailchimp for email, ClickFunnels for funnels, Teachable for courses, and Stripe for payments. It works — until it doesn't. The integrations break, data doesn't sync, and you spend Friday afternoons troubleshooting instead of coaching.

## What Exactly Is GoHighLevel SaaS Mode?

SaaS mode is GoHighLevel's built-in reseller infrastructure. When you activate it, your clients never see the GoHighLevel brand — they see your brand. You get a custom login URL (e.g., app.youragency.com), your own logo in the dashboard, and the ability to create client sub-accounts with specific feature sets enabled or disabled.

More importantly, SaaS mode connects directly to Stripe. You configure your pricing plans inside GoHighLevel, and when a client signs up through your branded portal, Stripe charges them automatically on a recurring basis. GoHighLevel handles the billing logic; you just collect the revenue.

This is fundamentally different from the standard Agency Unlimited plan ($297/mo), where you can create sub-accounts for clients but there's no built-in client-facing billing or white-label login portal. SaaS mode is what turns GoHighLevel from a tool you use into a product you sell.

## GoHighLevel SaaS Mode Pricing: What You Actually Pay

The Agency Pro plan costs $497/mo (or roughly $397/mo if you commit annually). That's your baseline cost. On top of that, you'll have usage costs:

- **Twilio** (SMS/calls): roughly $0.0075 per SMS segment, $0.0085/min for calls — you can pass these through to clients or bundle them in a usage allowance
- **Mailgun or SendGrid** (email): fractions of a cent per email at normal agency volumes
- **Stripe fees**: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction on client payments

Some agencies add a small markup on Twilio/Mailgun costs; others roll a usage allowance into their monthly price (e.g., "500 SMS included"). Both models work. The key point: your hard cost to operate the platform is predictable and relatively low.

| Cost Item | Approximate Monthly Cost |
|---|---|
| Agency Pro plan | $497 |
| Twilio (light usage, 5 clients) | $20–$60 |
| Mailgun (5 clients, moderate sends) | $5–$20 |

If you charge 5 clients $297/mo each, you're collecting $1,485 — a net margin of roughly $865/mo from five clients. That's before you add any service fees. Scale to 20 clients and the math becomes very interesting.

## How to Set Up SaaS Mode: The Core Steps

I'll be direct: setup takes 4–8 hours the first time if you're methodical. Here's the sequence I follow with clients:

**1. Upgrade to Agency Pro and activate SaaS mode**
Inside your agency account, go to Settings → Company → SaaS Configurator. Toggle SaaS mode on. You'll be prompted to connect Stripe.

**2. Connect Stripe**
This is non-negotiable. SaaS mode's automated billing runs entirely through Stripe. Connect your Stripe account (or create one — it takes 15 minutes to get approved for most businesses).

**3. Set up Twilio and Mailgun**
Create accounts on both platforms. Inside GoHighLevel's agency settings, add your Twilio Account SID and Auth Token, and your Mailgun API key. These power SMS, calls, and email for all your sub-accounts.

**4. Configure your white-label domain**
Point a subdomain (like app.youragency.com) to GoHighLevel's servers via a CNAME record. This is where your clients log in. GoHighLevel walks you through the DNS settings.

**5. Build your pricing plans**
In the SaaS Configurator, create the plans you'll offer — for example, a "Starter" plan at $197/mo with basic features, and a "Pro" plan at $397/mo with everything unlocked. For each plan, you control exactly which GoHighLevel features are visible to that tier.

**6. Create a snapshot for client onboarding**
A snapshot is a pre-built sub-account template — think funnels, automations, pipelines, and email templates relevant to your niche. When a new client signs up, you deploy the snapshot and they're operational in minutes instead of days. This is the single biggest time-saver in the whole SaaS model.

**7. Build your client sign-up funnel**
GoHighLevel lets you create a branded landing page where prospects can select a plan and pay directly through Stripe. Once they pay, a sub-account is auto-created and the snapshot is deployed. Done right, this is fully automated.

## Who Should (and Shouldn't) Use SaaS Mode

**SaaS mode is a great fit if you:**
- Already have 5+ clients or a warm audience you can pitch to
- Work in a specific niche (HVAC, real estate, dental, etc.) where you can build one strong snapshot and sell it repeatedly
- Want to shift from trading time for money to generating recurring revenue
- Have the patience to invest 4–8 hours in setup before seeing returns

**SaaS mode is NOT a good fit if you:**
- Are brand new with zero clients and no audience — you'll pay $497/mo while you figure out sales
- Serve enterprise clients who already have IT departments and won't accept a resold SaaS tool
- Want a simple tool to manage your own marketing — the Starter or Unlimited plan is more appropriate

My honest recommendation: start on Unlimited ($297/mo), get 3–5 clients using GoHighLevel's features, then upgrade to Agency Pro once you're confident in the value proposition and have warm prospects to pitch the software to.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q:** What plan do I need for GoHighLevel SaaS mode?
**A:** You need the Agency Pro plan, which costs $497/mo (or approximately $397/mo billed annually). SaaS mode is not available on the Starter ($97) or Unlimited ($297) plans.

**Q:** How does GoHighLevel handle client billing in SaaS mode?
**A:** GoHighLevel integrates directly with your Stripe account. You set up pricing plans in the SaaS Configurator, and when clients sign up through your branded portal, Stripe charges them automatically on a recurring schedule. You receive the funds in your Stripe account, not GoHighLevel's.

**Q:** Can clients see that the software is actually GoHighLevel?
**A:** Not if you set it up correctly. SaaS mode gives you a custom login URL (your own domain), your own logo, brand colors, and a fully white-labeled interface. GoHighLevel's branding is removed. That said, tech-savvy clients can sometimes identify the underlying platform through browser inspection, but for the vast majority of SMB clients, it's completely invisible.

**Q:** How long does SaaS mode setup take?
**A:** In my experience, the core technical setup — Stripe connection, Twilio, Mailgun, custom domain, and basic pricing plans — takes 4–8 hours. Building a high-quality onboarding snapshot for your niche can take another 10–20 hours but only needs to be done once. After that, client onboarding is largely automated.

**Q:** What's a realistic price to charge clients for white-labeled GoHighLevel?
**A:** Most agencies I've seen charge between $97/mo and $497/mo depending on the feature set and niche. A common starting point is $197–$297/mo for a core plan including CRM, pipelines, two-way SMS, email marketing, and basic automation. Higher-tier plans ($397–$497/mo) typically add more advanced automation, AI features, or usage allowances.

**Q:** Do I need a separate Twilio account for SaaS mode?
**A:** Yes. You connect your own Twilio account to your GoHighLevel agency, and each client sub-account uses your Twilio credentials (or you can enable clients to connect their own). You pay Twilio's rates directly and can mark them up or roll them into your subscription price.

**Q:** Is GoHighLevel SaaS mode worth it compared to building my own software?
**A:** Overwhelmingly yes, for most agencies. Building a comparable platform from scratch (CRM, funnel builder, email/SMS automation, pipelines, reputation management, booking, AI) would cost hundreds of thousands of dollars and years of development. At $497/mo, you're renting a production-ready platform with an active development team shipping features every week.

GoHighLevel and Kartra are both all-in-one marketing platforms, but they serve very different buyers — GoHighLevel is built for agencies and service businesses that need deep CRM functionality and client management at scale, while Kartra is designed around a simpler, creator-friendly funnel and membership experience. After implementing both for clients across different niches, my honest verdict is that GoHighLevel wins on breadth and agency economics, but Kartra holds its own for solopreneurs who want a polished, low-complexity setup.

## Key Takeaways

- **GoHighLevel** ($97–$497/mo) is the stronger choice for agencies, consultants managing multiple clients, and businesses that need two-way SMS, pipeline CRM, and white-label reselling.
- **Kartra** ($119–$549/mo) is better suited for solo course creators and coaches who want an opinionated, template-driven platform without a steep learning curve.
- GoHighLevel's SaaS mode lets agencies resell the platform under their own brand — Kartra has no equivalent.
- Kartra's built-in affiliate management and video hosting are genuinely polished; GoHighLevel's equivalents exist but feel secondary.
- Neither platform is a clear winner for everyone — your business model determines which one pays off.

## What Is Each Platform Actually Built For?

Before diving into feature tables, it's worth being honest about who engineered each product.

**GoHighLevel** was built from the ground up for marketing agencies. Every architectural decision — sub-accounts, snapshot cloning, white-label options, the SaaS reseller tier — reflects that origin. If you're running campaigns for clients, managing leads across multiple pipelines, or trying to replace a $1,200/month tool stack with one subscription, GHL is designed with you in mind.

**Kartra** launched in 2018 as an all-in-one for digital product sellers: courses, memberships, funnels, email sequences, checkout pages, and affiliate programs. It's opinionated in a good way — you get fewer configuration choices, which means less time setting up and more time selling. The tradeoff is that it doesn't stretch well outside that creator-focused use case.

I've implemented GoHighLevel for a cleaning company, a real estate team, and several marketing agencies. I've set up Kartra for a course creator and a membership site owner. The experience gap between those two contexts is significant.

## Pricing Breakdown: Which Platform Costs Less at Scale?

This is where context matters enormously.

**GoHighLevel pricing:**
- Starter: $97/mo (1 sub-account, core features)
- Unlimited: $297/mo (unlimited sub-accounts, white-label desktop app)
- Agency Pro / SaaS: $497/mo (full reseller mode, rebilling, SaaS configurator)

Usage-based costs (SMS, calls, email sends) are billed via Twilio/Mailgun integrations — I cover this in detail in [how much GoHighLevel really costs](/blog/gohighlevel-hidden-costs-real-cost).

**Kartra pricing (2026):**
- Starter: $119/mo (2,500 contacts, 1 custom domain)
- Growth: $229/mo (12,500 contacts, 3 domains)
- Professional: $549/mo (25,000 contacts, 5 domains)

Kartra is contact-limit based. If your list grows past 25,000 contacts, pricing jumps significantly. GoHighLevel has no contact limits — you pay for usage (emails sent, SMS messages), not list size.

For an agency managing 10 clients, GoHighLevel at $297/mo is dramatically cheaper than spinning up 10 Kartra accounts. For a solo creator with a list under 5,000 people, Kartra's $119 plan is reasonably priced and simpler to manage.

## Where GoHighLevel Clearly Wins

**Agency infrastructure.** There is no comparison here. GoHighLevel's sub-account system, snapshot cloning (where you can template an entire business setup and deploy it in minutes), and white-label options don't exist in Kartra. If you're managing more than one client, GHL saves you hundreds of dollars and dozens of hours monthly.

**Two-way SMS and conversations.** GHL's unified conversation inbox — handling SMS, email, Facebook DMs, Instagram DMs, and WhatsApp in one place — is genuinely useful for service businesses. Kartra has no SMS capability whatsoever.

**CRM depth.** The pipeline management, lead scoring, opportunity tracking, and automated follow-up sequences in GHL are built for actual sales workflows. Kartra's contact management is more of a list-tagging system than a real CRM.

**AI features.** GoHighLevel has invested heavily in AI in 2026 — conversation AI for chat widgets, AI voice for inbound call handling, content AI — features I've broken down in detail in the [GoHighLevel AI features overview](/blog/gohighlevel-ai-features-2026). Kartra's AI tooling is minimal by comparison.

**SaaS reselling.** At the $497/mo Agency Pro tier, you can resell GoHighLevel under your own brand, set your own pricing, and keep the margin. This is a fundamentally different business model that Kartra simply doesn't offer.

## Where Kartra Holds Its Ground

I want to be fair here, because Kartra genuinely does some things better.

**Built-in affiliate management.** Kartra's affiliate system is clean, functional, and integrated from day one. You can create multi-tier commission structures, generate affiliate links, pay via integrations, and track performance — all without third-party tools. GoHighLevel has an affiliate module, but it feels bolted on compared to Kartra's implementation.

**Native video hosting.** If you're building a course or membership site with heavy video content, Kartra's built-in video hosting (with progress tracking, tagging on play/completion, and gating) is a real advantage. GoHighLevel requires embedding from Vimeo, YouTube, or similar.

**Simpler onboarding for non-technical users.** GoHighLevel is powerful, but the learning curve is steep — especially if you're not coming from a marketing or agency background. Kartra's guided flow for building a funnel, checkout, and sequence is genuinely beginner-friendly. I've watched non-technical course creators get a working sales page and email sequence live in under two hours on Kartra. That same task in GHL would take most beginners a full day.

**Opinionated design templates.** Kartra's page templates are consistently better-looking out of the box. GHL's template library has grown, but Kartra's design polish is still a notch above.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q:** Is GoHighLevel better than Kartra for agencies?
**A:** Yes, unambiguously. GoHighLevel's sub-accounts, snapshot system, white-labeling, and SaaS reseller mode are purpose-built for agencies. Kartra has no multi-client architecture and isn't designed to serve that use case.

**Q:** Does Kartra have a CRM?
**A:** Kartra has contact management with tagging and segmentation, but it's not a full CRM. It lacks visual pipeline stages, deal tracking, and the kind of sales workflow management that GoHighLevel or a dedicated CRM provides.

**Q:** Which platform is cheaper, GoHighLevel or Kartra?
**A:** It depends on your use case. For a solo creator with a small list, Kartra's $119/mo Starter plan is competitive. For agencies managing multiple clients, GoHighLevel at $297/mo is significantly cheaper than running multiple Kartra accounts.

**Q:** Can I sell courses on GoHighLevel?
**A:** Yes. GoHighLevel has a course and membership builder, plus a communities feature. It works well, though Kartra's course experience — especially with native video hosting and completion tracking — is more polished for that specific use case.

**Q:** Does GoHighLevel have a free trial?
**A:** Yes, GoHighLevel offers a 14-day free trial. Kartra also offers a 14-day trial. Both let you explore the platform before committing.

**Q:** Can GoHighLevel replace Kartra entirely?
**A:** For most use cases, yes. The main exceptions are native video hosting (GHL requires third-party embeds) and affiliate management (Kartra's is more mature). If those two features are central to your business, Kartra may still have an edge.

**Q:** Which platform has better automation?
**A:** Both have visual automation builders. GoHighLevel's is more powerful for multi-channel workflows (SMS, email, calls, voicemail drops, webhooks). Kartra's is cleaner and easier to use for linear email and page-based sequences.

## Conclusion

After implementing both platforms across real client projects, my honest recommendation breaks down simply: **if you're an agency owner or service business managing multiple clients, GoHighLevel wins — and it's not particularly close.** The economics, the CRM depth, the two-way messaging, and the SaaS reseller option make it the obvious choice at $297/mo for unlimited client accounts.

If you're a solo digital product creator who wants a clean, guided experience with native video hosting and a solid affiliate program, **Kartra is a legitimate competitor** — especially at the lower contact tiers.

The good news is that both platforms offer a 14-day free trial. If you're on the fence and your business involves any kind of client management, lead follow-up, or multi-channel communication, I'd start with GoHighLevel's trial and see how quickly it replaces your existing tool stack.

GoHighLevel and Kartra are both marketed as "all-in-one" platforms, but in practice they serve very different types of businesses — and choosing the wrong one will cost you time, money, and a painful migration. After implementing both platforms across client projects, my honest take is that GoHighLevel is the stronger choice for agencies, consultants, and businesses managing multiple clients, while Kartra makes more sense for solo digital product sellers who prioritize a low-friction setup over CRM depth.

## Key Takeaways

- GoHighLevel offers true white-label agency capabilities; Kartra does not.
- Kartra's Starter plan ($99/mo) is actually comparable in price to GoHighLevel's Starter ($97/mo), but GoHighLevel includes features Kartra gates behind higher tiers.
- GoHighLevel's SaaS mode (Agency Pro at $497/mo) lets you resell the platform itself — Kartra has no equivalent.
- Kartra has a more polished native course/membership experience out of the box; GoHighLevel's membership module is functional but less refined.
- For agencies managing 2+ clients, GoHighLevel wins on scalability, automation depth, and total cost per seat.

## What Are These Platforms, Exactly?

GoHighLevel is an all-in-one sales and marketing platform built specifically with agencies in mind. It combines CRM, pipelines, email and SMS marketing, landing page builder, funnel builder, appointment scheduling, reputation management, AI tools, and a white-label reselling layer — all under one roof. I've used it across home services, real estate, coaching, and B2B agency clients.

Kartra is an all-in-one platform geared toward digital product creators and course sellers. It includes email marketing, landing pages, funnels, a video hosting tool, affiliate management, membership/course portals, and basic CRM functionality. It launched in 2018 and has steadily improved, but its DNA is still firmly rooted in the "info product" world.

The fundamental difference: GoHighLevel is CRM-first, automation-first, agency-first. Kartra is funnel-and-course-first, solo-creator-first.

## Pricing: How Do They Actually Compare?

Let's be specific, because both platforms have restructured pricing recently.

**GoHighLevel pricing (2026):**
- Starter: $97/mo — 1 account, all core features
- Unlimited: $297/mo — unlimited sub-accounts, white-label desktop app
- Agency Pro (SaaS): $497/mo — full SaaS reselling capability, split billing

**Kartra pricing (2026):**
- Starter: $99/mo — up to 2,500 contacts, 1 custom domain
- Growth: $189/mo — up to 12,500 contacts, 3 custom domains
- Professional: $429/mo — up to 25,000 contacts, 5 custom domains
- Enterprise: custom pricing

At first glance, Kartra's Starter looks similar to GoHighLevel's. But notice that Kartra gates features behind contact count and domain limits — the way most email platforms used to. GoHighLevel's Starter has no contact limit and no domain cap. If your list grows past 2,500 people, Kartra almost doubles your price to $189/mo before you've unlocked any agency capabilities.

## Where GoHighLevel Wins Clearly

**1. Agency and multi-client use**
If you're running an agency — even a small one with 3 to 5 clients — GoHighLevel is in a different league. Sub-accounts let each client have their own CRM, pipelines, automations, and even a branded portal. Kartra was never designed for this. There's no equivalent concept.

**2. CRM and sales pipeline depth**
GoHighLevel's CRM feels like a real CRM. Custom fields, lead scoring, pipeline stages, triggers based on deal movement, SMS follow-ups firing automatically when a lead goes cold — I've built this for cleaning companies, law offices, and real estate teams. Kartra's contact management is closer to a list tool than a true CRM.

**3. SMS, calling, and conversation management**
GoHighLevel has a unified inbox that pulls in email, SMS, Facebook Messenger, Instagram DMs, WhatsApp, and Google Business messages. It also has a native phone system. This is the feature that most impresses clients when I demo it. Kartra doesn't offer this.

**4. Reputation and local marketing tools**
For local businesses, GoHighLevel includes review request automation for Google and Facebook, which Kartra completely lacks. For agencies serving local clients — restaurants, med spas, contractors — this alone justifies the platform choice.

**5. SaaS reselling**
If you want to build a software business on top of GoHighLevel — setting up white-labeled accounts and charging clients a monthly SaaS fee — that's possible on the Agency Pro plan. I go deep on this model in [how agencies make money with GoHighLevel SaaS mode](/blog/how-agencies-make-money-gohighlevel-saas-mode). Kartra offers nothing like this.

## Where Kartra Holds Its Own

I said I'd be honest, and here's where Kartra genuinely competes or wins:

**Course and membership experience:** Kartra's membership portal is cleaner out of the box. Drip content, gated lessons, progress tracking — it all feels more purpose-built than GoHighLevel's membership module, which works but can require more setup to look polished.

**Native video hosting:** Kartra lets you host video directly on the platform and embed it with behavioral triggers ("if watched 80%, send this email"). GoHighLevel doesn't have native video hosting — you'd use YouTube, Vimeo, or Wistia. For course creators, this matters.

**Affiliate management (Kartra Marketplace):** Kartra has a built-in affiliate marketplace where people can find and promote your offer. GoHighLevel has an affiliate manager but no equivalent marketplace. If you rely heavily on affiliate-driven sales, this is a real differentiator for Kartra.

**Simplicity for solo creators:** If you sell one course or one membership, you're not running an agency, and you don't need CRM depth — Kartra's UX may feel less overwhelming initially. GoHighLevel has a steeper learning curve precisely because it does more.

## Who Should Choose Which Platform?

**Choose GoHighLevel if you:**
- Run a marketing or lead generation agency
- Manage clients across multiple accounts
- Need SMS automation, reputation management, or a unified inbox
- Want to white-label or resell the platform
- Serve local businesses (home services, real estate, health & wellness)
- Plan to grow past 1-2 clients

**Choose Kartra if you:**
- Sell digital courses or memberships as a solo creator
- Want native video hosting as a core feature
- Run a strong affiliate program and want marketplace exposure
- Don't need a real CRM or SMS capabilities
- Prefer a flatter learning curve with no agency overhead

For everything in-between — coaches who also want CRM, consultants managing a few clients, service businesses trying to automate follow-ups — GoHighLevel is the more future-proof investment. The $97 Starter plan already includes more practical automation than Kartra's equivalent tier, and if you scale, the upgrade path is clear.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q:** Is GoHighLevel or Kartra better for a beginner?
**A:** For beginners selling a single course or digital product, Kartra is simpler to start. For beginners building an agency or service business, GoHighLevel's learning curve pays off quickly because of its automation depth and client management tools.

**Q:** Can Kartra replace a CRM like HubSpot or GoHighLevel?
**A:** No. Kartra's contact management is adequate for simple email campaigns, but it lacks pipeline management, deal stages, conversation tracking, and SMS — the features that define a proper CRM. GoHighLevel is the stronger CRM choice.

**Q:** Does GoHighLevel have a free trial?
**A:** Yes, GoHighLevel offers a 14-day free trial on all plans. Kartra also offers a 30-day trial at $1. Both are worth testing with your actual workflows before committing.

**Q:** Which platform is cheaper for a solo entrepreneur?
**A:** At the entry level they're nearly identical — GoHighLevel Starter at $97/mo vs Kartra Starter at $99/mo. However, Kartra's price scales with contact count, so as your list grows, GoHighLevel becomes the better value since it doesn't charge based on contacts.

**Q:** Does Kartra offer white-labeling?
**A:** No. Kartra does not offer white-label or reseller capabilities. If you need to present a branded software product to clients, only GoHighLevel supports this — starting with the Unlimited plan at $297/mo.

**Q:** Can I run an affiliate program on GoHighLevel?
**A:** Yes, GoHighLevel includes an affiliate manager where you can create programs, set commissions, and generate tracking links. However, it doesn't have a public marketplace like Kartra's. If affiliate discoverability is critical to your business model, Kartra has an edge there.

**Q:** Which platform has better automation workflows?
**A:** GoHighLevel's visual workflow builder is more powerful and flexible — it supports complex branching logic, wait conditions, SMS, email, internal notifications, pipeline movements, and AI steps. Kartra's automations are solid for email-based sequences but don't match GoHighLevel's depth.

## Conclusion

After using both platforms in real client work, my conclusion is straightforward: GoHighLevel is the better platform for the majority of business owners reading this. If you're an agency, a consultant, or a service business that needs a real CRM with automation, SMS, and the ability to scale — GoHighLevel wins the comparison decisively. Kartra is a legitimate tool, but its sweet spot is narrow: solo digital product creators who prioritize course delivery and affiliate marketing over CRM and client management.

The 14-day free trial is enough time to build a real pipeline, set up automations, and test the unified inbox — I'd strongly recommend doing that before committing to either platform. You'll know within a week whether GoHighLevel fits your workflow.

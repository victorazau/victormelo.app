## What Does a Real Estate CRM Actually Need to Do?

A CRM for real estate agents isn't just a contact database. In 2026, a competitive real estate CRM needs to handle all of the following without requiring a tech team to glue things together:

- **Lead capture** from Zillow, Realtor.com, Facebook Ads, and your own website
- **Automated follow-up** via SMS, email, and voicemail drops — because 78% of sales go to the first responder
- **Pipeline visibility** so you know exactly where every buyer and seller lead sits
- **Appointment scheduling** integrated into your calendar
- **Long-term drip campaigns** for leads that won't buy for 6–18 months
- **Two-way texting** because agents live on their phones

Most single-purpose tools do 3–4 of these things. GoHighLevel does all of them — which is why I keep recommending it to agents who are serious about scaling.

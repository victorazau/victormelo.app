GoHighLevel and GetResponse are both marketed as all-in-one platforms, but they serve very different users and solve very different problems. GoHighLevel is built for agencies and service businesses that need a full CRM, two-way SMS, sales pipelines, and white-label capability — GetResponse started as an email marketing tool and has since layered on automation, landing pages, and webinars. If you're deciding between the two, the short answer is this: if you run an agency or manage clients, GoHighLevel wins. If you're a solo creator or small e-commerce brand on a tight budget who mostly needs email, GetResponse is a legitimate option.

## Key Takeaways

- GoHighLevel starts at $97/month and replaces 8–12 tools; GetResponse starts at $19/month but requires additional tools for a full marketing stack.
- GoHighLevel includes a built-in CRM, two-way SMS, phone calls, and reputation management — none of which GetResponse offers natively.
- GetResponse has a longer track record in email deliverability and a more polished email builder for pure email marketers.
- GoHighLevel's white-label and SaaS reseller features have no equivalent in GetResponse.
- For agencies managing multiple client accounts, GoHighLevel is the clear winner. For individual bloggers or e-commerce stores focused on email list building, GetResponse can be cheaper to start.

## What Is Each Platform, Exactly?

**GoHighLevel** is an all-in-one sales and marketing platform designed primarily for marketing agencies and local service businesses. At its core it combines a CRM, pipeline management, multi-channel automation (email, SMS, voice, Facebook Messenger, WhatsApp), website and funnel builder, appointment scheduling, review management, and a white-label option that lets agencies resell the entire platform under their own brand. I've implemented it across dozens of client accounts in industries ranging from home services to coaching, and the breadth of what's included under one login is genuinely hard to match.

**GetResponse** is primarily an email marketing platform that has expanded over the years. It now includes marketing automation workflows, landing pages, a website builder, webinars, e-commerce integrations, and paid ads tools. It's well-suited for content creators, bloggers, small e-commerce brands, and solopreneurs who want to grow and monetize an email list without needing a full CRM or client management system.

These two products overlap in email marketing and basic funnels. Everything else is a different game entirely.

## Pricing: What Do You Actually Pay?

GetResponse pricing at a glance:
- **Free plan**: Up to 500 contacts, very limited features
- **Email Marketing**: $19/month (1,000 contacts)
- **Marketing Automation**: $59/month
- **E-commerce Marketing**: $119/month
- Prices scale steeply as your contact list grows — at 10,000 contacts, the Marketing Automation plan jumps to $95/month

GoHighLevel pricing:
- **Starter**: $97/month — 1 account, all core features, unlimited contacts
- **Unlimited**: $297/month — unlimited sub-accounts, white-label desktop app
- **Agency Pro (SaaS)**: $497/month — full SaaS mode, resell GoHighLevel as your own product

Here's what I tell every client who balks at GoHighLevel's price versus GetResponse's: GetResponse at $19/month gives you email marketing and landing pages. To run a real agency or service business, you'd still need a CRM (HubSpot: $90–$800/month), a scheduling tool (Calendly: $16/month), a review management tool ($50–$200/month), and an SMS platform (Twilio or SimpleTexting: $30+/month). That stack runs $200–$1,100/month easily. GoHighLevel consolidates all of that for $97.

## Email Marketing Quality: Where GetResponse Still Has an Edge

I'll be honest here because honesty is the whole point of this blog: GetResponse has been doing email marketing since 1998. Their deliverability infrastructure, template library, and A/B testing tools are mature and polished in a way that GoHighLevel's email module isn't quite at yet.

If your entire business model is growing and monetizing an email newsletter — think content creator, blogger, or affiliate marketer — GetResponse's email experience feels more native and refined. The template editor is cleaner, the deliverability reporting is more detailed, and features like the AI email generator and pre-built automation flows are well-thought-out.

GoHighLevel's email is perfectly functional for sending campaigns and automated sequences as part of a broader workflow. But it wasn't built to be an email-first tool. It was built to orchestrate multi-channel communication — and that's where it genuinely wins.

For agencies and service businesses, this distinction rarely matters. For a solopreneur building a list as their primary growth channel, it's worth considering.

## Automation: Which Platform Goes Deeper?

Both platforms offer visual automation builders. GoHighLevel's workflow builder is more powerful in terms of triggers and actions — you can trigger automations based on missed calls, form submissions, pipeline stage changes, appointment bookings, SMS replies, and more. You can also connect to any channel: email, SMS, voice, social DMs, and Slack-style internal notifications.

GetResponse's automation is email-centric. It's excellent for list segmentation, behavioral triggers based on email opens and clicks, and e-commerce events like abandoned cart sequences. It integrates with Shopify, WooCommerce, and Magento natively, which makes it solid for e-commerce brands.

In my experience implementing both, GoHighLevel's automation depth wins for service businesses because the triggers extend beyond email behavior to real-world business events — a call came in, a lead didn't show up for an appointment, a review was left. GetResponse stays squarely in the digital engagement lane.

## Who Should Choose GetResponse?

GetResponse is the right choice if you:
- Are a solopreneur, blogger, or content creator building an email list as your primary channel
- Run a simple e-commerce store and need email + abandoned cart sequences
- Host webinars as a core part of your business (GetResponse's webinar tool is solid and native)
- Are early-stage and genuinely can't justify $97/month yet
- Don't need a CRM, two-way SMS, or agency client management

I'll give credit where it's due: GetResponse at $19/month with 1,000 contacts is a legitimate entry point for someone just getting started. It's not a scam or an inferior product — it's just a different product built for a different user.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q:** Is GoHighLevel better than GetResponse for email marketing?
**A:** For pure email marketing volume and deliverability refinement, GetResponse has a slight edge due to its longer history and more polished email-specific tooling. However, GoHighLevel is better for businesses that use email as one channel within a broader CRM-driven workflow.

**Q:** Can GetResponse replace a CRM?
**A:** No. GetResponse does not include a CRM, sales pipeline, or contact management system equivalent to what GoHighLevel offers. You would need a separate CRM tool alongside GetResponse.

**Q:** How much does GoHighLevel cost compared to GetResponse?
**A:** GoHighLevel starts at $97/month for its Starter plan. GetResponse starts at $19/month for email marketing, scaling up to $59–$119/month for automation and e-commerce features. However, GoHighLevel includes a full CRM, SMS, and scheduling tools that GetResponse does not, making direct price comparisons misleading.

**Q:** Does GetResponse have a white-label option for agencies?
**A:** No. GetResponse does not offer white-labeling or a reseller/SaaS mode. GoHighLevel's Unlimited ($297/month) and Agency Pro ($497/month) plans include full white-label capability, allowing agencies to rebrand and resell the platform.

**Q:** Does GoHighLevel have a free trial?
**A:** Yes, GoHighLevel offers a 14-day free trial. GetResponse offers a 30-day free trial and a limited free plan for up to 500 contacts.

**Q:** Which platform is better for coaches?
**A:** GoHighLevel is generally better for coaches who also manage client pipelines, booking, and follow-up sequences. GetResponse can work for coaches whose business is primarily course sales and email list monetization. For a deeper look at coaching-specific platforms, I covered GoHighLevel against a direct coaching rival in the GoHighLevel vs Kajabi comparison.

**Q:** Can I use GoHighLevel for webinars?
**A:** GoHighLevel does not have a native webinar tool as of 2026. You'd need to integrate with Zoom or another webinar platform. GetResponse includes webinar hosting natively, which is one area where it genuinely wins.

## Conclusion

After using and implementing both platforms, my honest verdict is this: GetResponse is a solid, mature email marketing tool that's genuinely useful for a specific type of user — the solopreneur or content creator who lives and breathes their email list. If that's you, it's worth considering.

But for agencies, consultants, and service businesses? GoHighLevel isn't even a close call. The CRM, two-way SMS, appointment scheduling, reputation management, sales pipelines, and white-label reseller capability combine into a platform that would cost you $400–$1,000/month to replicate with individual tools. At $97/month on the Starter plan, it's genuinely one of the best value propositions in the marketing software space.

If you're on the fence, GoHighLevel's 14-day free trial is long enough to build out a real workflow and see whether it fits your business. There's no better way to decide than to test it yourself.

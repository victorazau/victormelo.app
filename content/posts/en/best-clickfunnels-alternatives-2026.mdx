If you're shopping for ClickFunnels alternatives in 2026, you already know the platform's reputation: powerful funnel builder, massive community, but a price tag that's hard to justify when the tool stack it replaces has mostly been commoditized. After two years implementing marketing systems for agency clients, I can tell you confidently that ClickFunnels is no longer the default choice — and for most businesses, there's a better fit at a lower cost.

## Key Takeaways

- ClickFunnels 2.0 starts at $97/mo but quickly jumps to $297/mo for features most businesses actually need
- GoHighLevel offers more features (CRM, SMS, reputation management, white-labeling) at the same or lower price point
- Systeme.io is the best free/budget option for solopreneurs building simple funnels
- Kartra and Kajabi are strong alternatives if your focus is courses + memberships
- The "best" alternative depends on whether you're an agency, a coach, an e-commerce brand, or a solo operator

## Why Are People Leaving ClickFunnels in 2026?

ClickFunnels built its reputation on simplicity: drag-and-drop funnels, one-click upsells, and Russell Brunson's marketing ecosystem. That formula worked brilliantly from 2015 to 2020. By 2026, the landscape looks different.

The ClickFunnels 2.0 rebuild was supposed to modernize everything, but the migration experience frustrated a large share of the existing user base. Page load speeds, while improved, still lag behind native alternatives. More critically, ClickFunnels is still primarily a funnel builder — when you need a CRM, email automation, SMS, and reputation management, you're stitching together external tools. That gets expensive fast.

I've had clients paying $297/mo for ClickFunnels plus $150/mo for ActiveCampaign plus $50/mo for a review tool. We consolidated all of that into a single platform and cut their monthly spend by over $200 while adding features they didn't even know they needed.

## The 6 Best ClickFunnels Alternatives in 2026

### 1. GoHighLevel — Best for Agencies and Growth-Focused Businesses

GoHighLevel is the alternative I recommend most often, and not because of the affiliate commission — I was using it with clients before I ever thought about writing about it. The platform combines CRM, funnel builder, email marketing, SMS automation, reputation management, appointment scheduling, and a white-label website builder under one roof.

For agencies specifically, the math is straightforward: the Unlimited plan at $297/mo covers unlimited client sub-accounts. You're not paying per client. Layer on the [SaaS mode at $497/mo](/blog/how-agencies-make-money-gohighlevel-saas-mode) and you can resell the platform to your own clients under your brand, turning it into a recurring revenue stream.

**Where GoHighLevel wins over ClickFunnels:**
- Built-in CRM with pipeline management (ClickFunnels has basic contacts, not a real CRM)
- Two-way SMS and email automation baked in
- Reputation management and Google Review requests
- Booking/calendar system comparable to Calendly
- White-label option for agencies
- 14-day free trial with no credit card required on most plans

**Where ClickFunnels still wins:**
- Russell Brunson's training ecosystem (OFA Challenge, etc.) if you're a beginner
- Slightly more polished out-of-the-box funnel templates for info products
- Larger community of funnel hackers sharing pre-built assets

**GoHighLevel pricing:** Starter $97/mo, Unlimited $297/mo, Agency Pro (SaaS) $497/mo

### 2. Systeme.io — Best Free Alternative for Solopreneurs

Systeme.io offers a genuinely free plan that includes funnels, email marketing, and basic course hosting. For a solopreneur launching their first offer, it's hard to argue with $0/mo. The paid plans top out at $97/mo for unlimited everything.

The trade-offs are real though. The CRM capabilities are minimal, automation is simpler than what GoHighLevel or even ActiveCampaign offer, and there's no native SMS. If your business grows beyond a few hundred contacts and a couple of funnels, you'll feel the ceiling.

**Best for:** Beginners, budget-conscious solopreneurs, simple one-product funnels

### 3. Kartra — Best for Course Creators Who Want One Tool

Kartra has quietly become a strong all-in-one for digital product sellers. It combines funnels, email, memberships, video hosting, affiliate management, and a help desk. The interface has improved significantly since its early days.

The downside is price — Kartra starts at $119/mo and scales steeply — and it lacks the agency-level features (white-labeling, multi-location management, SMS) that GoHighLevel offers. If you're a solo coach or course creator with no interest in managing client accounts, Kartra deserves a serious look.

**Best for:** Course creators, coaches, digital product businesses

### 4. Kajabi — Best for Premium Course and Community Businesses

Kajabi is the gold standard for high-ticket course creators who want beautiful branding and a seamless student experience. The community features, podcast hosting, and coaching tools are genuinely excellent. But Kajabi starts at $149/mo and doesn't include a real funnel builder in the ClickFunnels sense — you're building landing pages, not multi-step conversion sequences.

I've written a full breakdown of [GoHighLevel vs Kajabi for coaches](/blog/gohighlevel-vs-kajabi-for-coaches) if that comparison is relevant to your situation.

**Best for:** Premium course creators, community builders, established coaches

### 5. GetResponse — Best Email-First Alternative with Funnel Features

GetResponse added funnel builder functionality to its core email marketing product a few years ago. It's a legitimate option if email is your primary channel and funnels are secondary. Pricing starts lower than ClickFunnels, and the email deliverability is solid.

The funnel builder feels like an add-on rather than a core product, which it essentially is. Don't choose GetResponse if funnels are central to your growth strategy.

**Best for:** Email-first marketers who need basic funnel pages

### 6. Builderall — Most Features Per Dollar (With Caveats)

Builderall tries to do everything and largely succeeds in theory. In practice, many of the tools feel half-finished and the interface has a learning curve that punishes beginners. I've seen agencies use it successfully, but it requires more patience than most people have.

**Best for:** Tech-comfortable marketers who want maximum features at minimum cost

## Which ClickFunnels Alternative Is Right for You?

The honest answer depends on your business model:

**You're a marketing agency or consultant:** GoHighLevel is the obvious choice. The ability to run unlimited client sub-accounts on one plan, combined with SMS, CRM, and white-labeling, makes it the most economically sensible platform at scale. If you want to understand the full economics, check out my breakdown of the [best all-in-one CRM for agencies in 2026](/blog/best-all-in-one-crm-for-agencies-2026).

**You're a solo coach or course creator:** Kajabi or Kartra give you a more polished student experience. GoHighLevel can do it, but course hosting is not its strongest suit. If budget matters more than polish, Systeme.io is genuinely impressive for $0.

**You're an e-commerce business:** None of these tools replace Shopify for serious e-commerce. ClickFunnels and GoHighLevel both work for supplement funnels and physical product launches, but if you need inventory management, native shipping integrations, and a product catalog, you're in a different category.

**You're a local service business (dentist, HVAC, law firm, etc.):** GoHighLevel was essentially built for this use case. The CRM, appointment booking, review requests, and two-way SMS are precisely what local businesses need. ClickFunnels was designed for info product marketers — the fit is awkward for service businesses.

## What Does the Switch Actually Cost?

One thing people underestimate is migration cost. Moving from ClickFunnels to any alternative means rebuilding funnels, re-integrating payment processors, and potentially migrating contact lists. Budget 5–10 hours of setup time for a simple migration, more if you have complex automations.

GoHighLevel offers done-for-you migration support on higher plans, and there are plenty of implementation specialists (like me) who do this regularly. The migration pain is real but temporary; the monthly savings are permanent.

For a solo operator moving from ClickFunnels Pro ($297/mo) to GoHighLevel Unlimited ($297/mo), the cost is identical on paper — but GoHighLevel includes CRM, SMS, and booking tools that most ClickFunnels users pay for separately. The effective savings are often $150–$300/mo.

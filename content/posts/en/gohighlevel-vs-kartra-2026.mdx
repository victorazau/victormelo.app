GoHighLevel and Kartra are both all-in-one marketing platforms, but they're built for very different buyers — GoHighLevel is purpose-built for agencies managing multiple clients, while Kartra is designed around a single business selling products, courses, or services online. After implementing both for clients, my honest take is that the right choice depends almost entirely on whether you're running an agency or running a solo business.

## Key Takeaways

- **GoHighLevel** starts at $97/mo and includes unlimited contacts on its Starter plan; Kartra's entry plan caps contacts and emails.
- **Kartra** has a more polished course and membership experience out of the box; GoHighLevel's course module is functional but less refined.
- **GoHighLevel** is the clear winner for agencies: white-label, sub-accounts, and SaaS reselling are built into the platform's DNA.
- **Kartra** suits solopreneurs and small info-product businesses that don't need CRM depth or multi-client management.
- Neither platform is perfect — I'll tell you exactly where each one falls short so you don't get surprised after you've committed.

## What Are GoHighLevel and Kartra?

GoHighLevel is a CRM and marketing automation platform launched in 2018, designed specifically for digital marketing agencies. It combines CRM pipelines, email and SMS marketing, funnel and website builders, appointment scheduling, reputation management, and a full AI layer — all under one roof. The standout feature is its agency architecture: you manage unlimited client sub-accounts from a single dashboard, and on the Agency Pro plan ($497/mo) you can white-label the entire platform and resell it as your own SaaS product.

Kartra is a marketing platform founded in 2018 by Genesis Digital (the same team behind WebinarJam). It's built around the concept of a single business selling online — funnels, email sequences, membership sites, video hosting, helpdesk, and affiliate management are all included. Kartra's strength is that it genuinely feels like a cohesive product. The pieces talk to each other well, and the learning curve for a non-technical user is relatively gentle.

The core tension: GoHighLevel gives you more *business types* and more *clients* per account. Kartra gives you a more polished experience for *one* business selling *one* product catalog.

## Features Compared: Where Each Platform Wins

### CRM and Pipeline Management

GoHighLevel wins here, and it's not close. The CRM is the heart of the platform — you get visual pipelines, smart lists, conversation routing across SMS, email, Instagram DMs, Facebook Messenger, and live chat, all in a unified inbox. I've set up pipelines for home service companies, law firms, and real estate teams, and the level of automation you can build around lead movement is genuinely impressive.

Kartra has a basic CRM layer — you can tag contacts, track lead scores, and trigger automations — but it's clearly secondary to the email and funnel functionality. If your business lives or dies by pipeline management, Kartra isn't the right tool.

### Funnels and Landing Pages

This is closer. Both platforms have drag-and-drop funnel builders and decent template libraries. Kartra's page builder feels slightly more polished and has better built-in split testing at lower tiers. GoHighLevel's builder is powerful, especially when you factor in the website builder and blog functionality, but it has a steeper learning curve for new users.

For simple funnel-and-email-sequence setups, Kartra is genuinely easier to launch fast. For complex multi-step automations tied to CRM stages, GoHighLevel is the better choice.

### Courses and Memberships

Kartra has the edge here. The membership portal builder is more visually polished, and the video hosting integration feels native. GoHighLevel added a courses module and it works, but experienced course creators will notice it's less refined — limited drip scheduling options, fewer design templates, and no native video hosting (you embed from YouTube or Vimeo).

If your core product is an online course or membership community and you don't need multi-client management, Kartra is the better experience for your students.

### Email and SMS Marketing

GoHighLevel covers both email and SMS natively. SMS is baked into the workflow builder and the unified inbox, which is a huge advantage for service businesses that rely on appointment reminders and follow-up sequences. Kartra is email-only — if you need SMS, you're looking at a third-party integration.

For agencies managing service-based clients (HVAC, med spas, dentists, gyms), GoHighLevel's SMS capability alone often justifies the switch.

### Agency and Multi-Client Management

This is GoHighLevel's defining advantage. The sub-account architecture lets you manage 10, 50, or 200 client accounts from one login, with separate branding, pipelines, and automations per client. On the SaaS mode ($497/mo), you can white-label the entire platform, set your own pricing tiers, and collect recurring revenue from clients who never know GoHighLevel exists under the hood.

Kartra has no equivalent. It's built for one business, one brand. If you're an agency, Kartra simply isn't in the conversation. I've written a full breakdown on [how agencies make money with GoHighLevel's SaaS mode](/blog/how-agencies-make-money-gohighlevel-saas-mode) if you want to understand the revenue potential there.

### AI Features

GoHighLevel has invested heavily in AI in 2025-2026: AI-powered conversation bots, voice AI for inbound calls, AI content generation, and sentiment analysis in the inbox. Kartra has added some AI copy tools but nothing at the same breadth or depth. If AI automation is part of your pitch to clients, GoHighLevel is ahead.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q:** Is GoHighLevel cheaper than Kartra?
**A:** At the entry level, GoHighLevel's Starter plan ($97/mo) is cheaper than Kartra's Starter ($119/mo) and gives you unlimited contacts vs Kartra's 2,500 contact cap. However, GoHighLevel charges separately for email sends via LC Email, so your real cost depends on volume. For most small businesses, GoHighLevel ends up cheaper or comparable.

**Q:** Can Kartra replace a CRM like GoHighLevel?
**A:** Not for agency use cases. Kartra has contact tagging and basic automation, but it lacks visual pipeline management, a unified multi-channel inbox, and any multi-client account structure. For solopreneurs selling a single product, it's sufficient. For agencies or service businesses needing real CRM depth, GoHighLevel is the better tool.

**Q:** Does Kartra have SMS marketing?
**A:** No, Kartra does not have native SMS marketing. You would need to integrate a third-party tool. GoHighLevel includes SMS natively in all plans, which is a significant advantage for service businesses that rely on text-based follow-ups and appointment reminders.

**Q:** Which platform is better for online courses?
**A:** Kartra has a more polished course and membership builder with better design templates and native video hosting awareness. GoHighLevel's course module works but is less refined. If courses are your primary product, Kartra gives your students a better experience. If courses are one feature among many (CRM, funnels, SMS), GoHighLevel handles it adequately.

**Q:** Can I white-label Kartra for my agency clients?
**A:** No. Kartra does not offer white-labeling or a reseller program. GoHighLevel's Agency Pro/SaaS plan ($497/mo) allows full white-labeling — your clients see your brand, not GoHighLevel's.

**Q:** What is GoHighLevel's free trial period?
**A:** GoHighLevel offers a 14-day free trial. Kartra also offers a 14-day trial for $1. Both give you access to the core features, so you can test each platform before committing.

**Q:** Which platform has better automation workflows?
**A:** GoHighLevel's workflow builder is more powerful for complex, multi-channel automations that span SMS, email, calls, pipeline stages, and AI bots. Kartra's automation is solid for email-centric sequences but doesn't match GoHighLevel's breadth when you need to coordinate across channels and CRM events simultaneously.

## Conclusion

After comparing both platforms hands-on, the decision isn't complicated if you're honest about what your business actually needs. GoHighLevel is the right choice for agencies, consultants managing multiple clients, and service businesses that need real CRM functionality, SMS automation, and the option to scale into a SaaS reseller model. Kartra is the right choice for solopreneurs and small info-product businesses that want a clean, self-contained platform for funnels, email, and course delivery — without the complexity of multi-client management.

The mistake I see most often is agencies trying to use Kartra because it looks polished, then hitting a wall when they need to spin up a second client account or set up SMS follow-ups. Conversely, I've seen solo course creators sign up for GoHighLevel and feel overwhelmed by features they'll never use.

If you're an agency or serious about scaling a service business, GoHighLevel is the clear winner here. The 14-day free trial is the right place to start — build one real workflow, import a test contact list, and see how it fits your actual operation before you commit to any annual plan.
